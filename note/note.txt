Step 14 — K-Means

Step 15 — Choose the number of clusters

Step 16 — Validate K=4 vs K=5
Silhouette score ranges from approximately -1 to +1.
Think of it this way:
Higher positive value → customers are generally well separated
Around 0 → clusters overlap
Negative → some customers may be assigned to the wrong/nearby cluster
Calculate the silhouette score for:
K = 2 - 6

Step 17 — Finalize K=5
Your five groups are:
Cluster 0 → high AOV, low frequency
Cluster 1 → active regular customers
Cluster 2 → moderate customers
Cluster 3 → discount-oriented, low activity
Cluster 4 → highly active customers

Conclusion
PCA reduced 8 customer behavior variables to 3 principal components explaining 81.75% of the variance. K-Means clustering was then applied to identify customer behavior segments. Although K=2 produced the highest silhouette score (0.3991), K=5 provided more detailed and interpretable behavioral profiles for business analysis. The resulting segments included Highly Engaged VIP, Active Loyal, Regular Moderate, High-Value Occasional, and Discount-Driven customers.
